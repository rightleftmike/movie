REWATCH CLUBPrivacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

The short version: your ratings and what you write about films are yours. You can download all of it or delete all of it yourself, from Settings, without asking anyone. We don't sell anything, we don't run ads, and there are no tracking cookies.

1. Who runs this

Rewatch Club is a fan-built film-tracking app, run by an individual in California. There is no company behind it — it is not incorporated, and it has no employees. It is an unofficial companion to a podcast about films and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by that podcast, its hosts, or its publishers.

Questions about this policy or your data: rightleftmike@gmail.com. There is also a feedback box in Settings that goes to the same person.

2. What we collect

  • Your email address, because that is how you sign in. You either receive a one-time sign-in link, or you sign in with Google, in which case Google gives us your name, email and profile photo. There are no passwords — we never create, store or see one.
  • A display name, if you choose to set one.
  • What you record about films: your rating, whether you'd rewatch, how many times you've seen it, the year you first saw it, and anything you write in the free-text fields ("what stuck with me", "standout performance", "what didn't click"). Older accounts may also have text in fields that have since been retired.
  • Your watchlist and the people you follow (actors, writers, directors).
  • A "taste profile" — a short description of your film taste, generated by AI from the above, stored against your account and used only for your own recommendations.
  • AI usage records: which feature you used, when, how many tokens it consumed and what it cost. This exists so weekly limits can be enforced and so the person paying the bill can see the bill.
  • Anything you send through the feedback box.
  • Counts of clicks on links out to Spotify, Apple Podcasts or Netflix. Nobody pays us for these; we just want to know whether the feature is used.

We do not collect your address, phone number, date of birth, government ID, or payment card details.

3. What we do with it

  • Run your account and show you your own films.
  • Generate recommendations. Your ratings and the text you write are sent to Google's Gemini models, through a server we control, to build your taste profile and produce suggestions. This happens for your account only. What you write is never shown to other users and never influences anyone else's recommendations.
  • Enforce weekly AI limits so one account can't exhaust the shared budget.
  • Understand in aggregate whether features are used.
  • Reply to you if you write to us.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use what you write for advertising. We do not send marketing email.

5. Analytics, cookies and tracking

Analytics are configured about as conservatively as the tool allows, and these are verifiable facts about the app rather than promises:

  • No tracking cookies. Analytics data is held in memory for the page session only.
  • No session recording. We cannot watch a replay of your screen.
  • No automatic capture. Only a specific, named list of events is recorded — for example that a film was rated, not what you wrote about it.
  • Do Not Track is respected. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, no analytics events are sent at all.

There is currently no separate opt-out switch inside Settings. Enabling Do Not Track in your browser is the way to opt out, and it works.

6. Your rights

  • Export. Settings → Your data → Export everything to JSON. Immediate, no request needed.
  • Delete. Settings → Delete everything. This removes your ratings, what you wrote, your watchlist, follows, taste profile, usage records and the account itself. It happens immediately and cannot be undone — there is no backup we can restore you from. Export first if you want a copy.
  • Correct. Edit or delete any individual rating at any time.
  • Ask. Email us and we'll answer.

If you are in California, the EU or the UK you have additional rights under CCPA/CPRA, the GDPR or the UK GDPR — including access, correction, deletion and portability. The export and delete controls above are built to satisfy those directly and without a waiting period. Write to us for anything they don't cover.

7. How long we keep it

Until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry. If you delete your account the data is gone at once. Records of payments may persist in Stripe's systems where law requires them to be retained.

8. Security

Every table holding your data uses row-level security, so one account cannot read another's. Sensitive server operations run only with server-side credentials that are never exposed to the browser. We never receive card details. No password exists to be stolen, because sign-in is by emailed link or Google.

No system is perfectly secure, and this one is maintained by one person. Please don't store anything in a film note that you would be harmed by losing or exposing.

9. Children

Rewatch Club is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and a notice appears in the app. Continuing to use it after that means you accept the new version.
