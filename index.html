Credits are spent by Suggest, Watch Tonight, note polishing and film stills. Everyone gets 50 a week — no card, no tiers, no account to upgrade. There's also a shared daily pool across all users, so on a busy day the AI can run dry before your own credits do. Rating films, notes, watchlist, follows and the episode list never touch credits and never run out.

How this is paid for

Rewatch Club is free — no ads, no memberships, nothing to buy. The AI features run on a fixed pot of credit that expires on 20 October 2026, and when it's gone they'll switch off unless I find a way to keep paying for them. I'd rather say that now than surprise you later.

Everything else — your ratings, notes, watchlist, follows and the episode list — keeps working regardless and stays free.

Rewatch Club runs in the browser — there's nothing to download and no app store. Adding it to your home screen gives it its own icon and opens it full screen, without the browser bars.
Import from Letterboxdratings, reviews, watchlist
Already track films on Letterboxd? Bring across everything that overlaps with the show — ratings, how many times you've seen each one, when you first saw it, and anything you wrote.
  1. On Letterboxd, go to Settings → Data → Export your data. It's free.
  2. Unzip the file it downloads.
  3. Pick the CSVs below — ratings, diary, reviews and watchlist. You can select all of them at once.
Your file is read in this browser and never uploaded. Nothing is saved until you've seen exactly what will change and pressed the button.

Permanently removes your ratings, notes, watchlist, follows, taste profile and account. This cannot be undone, and there is no backup we can restore from — export first if you want a copy.
